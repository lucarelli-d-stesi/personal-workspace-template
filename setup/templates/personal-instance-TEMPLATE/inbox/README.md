# Inbox (`inbox/`)

The **Inbox** is a temporary staging ground for fast capture and unreviewed lessons learned.

## Workflow

1. **Quick Capture**: Drop rough thoughts, meeting notes, links, or quick ideas here without worrying about perfect classification.
2. **Weekly Review**: During the weekly review routine (`knowledge/playbooks/weekly-review.md`), items in `inbox/` are processed:
   - Actionable tasks move to `backlog/items/`.
   - General insights move to `knowledge/`.
   - Ephemeral notes are discarded.
